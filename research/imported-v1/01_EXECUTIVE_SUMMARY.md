# Rumelia Atlas v1 pilot: Skopje–Tetovo–Prizren

This first pilot release is an evidence-backed research package, not a completed historical atlas. It covers the Skopje/Üsküp–Tetovo/Kalkandelen–Prizren corridor and a small adjoining frame (Prishtina, Vushtrri, Kaçanik, Ferizaj, Rahovec, Gostivar, Kumanovo and Bresanë).

## What is successfully researched

The pilot establishes a controlled gazetteer with 14 place entities and multilingual name variants; dated Ottoman administrative affiliations from 1392 through the late Ottoman period; traceable demographic records from 1452/53, 1467/68, 1477, 1569, 1833 and 2021; 31 heritage records including extant, converted, reconstructed, ruined and explicitly lost components; 10 migration records; 15 historical-map records; and 13 GIS-source records.

The strongest evidence chain is the published Ottoman register literature and source editions (TD 370/TD 167, BOA NFS.d.5624, BOA ML.VRD.TMT.15465), the İSAM Kosova Vilayeti salname scans, the North Macedonia State Archives publication catalogue, the Kosovo Cultural Heritage Database, the North Macedonia 2021 census, and the cited scholarship by Zorba, Sulimani, İnbaşı, Katıć, Jagodić/Kule, Jovanović and migration studies.

## Main findings

Tetovo is documented as Htetova/IhTetova, a village-marketplace in 1452/53, then a city and administrative-military centre by 1467/68. The source literature reports Muslim and Christian household/family categories, but does not justify ethnic totals. Skopje's 1833 NFS.d.5624 summary records Muslim, Christian, Yahudiyân and Kıbtî categories; none is silently converted to Turkish, Albanian, Macedonian or Roma. Prizren's 15th- and 16th-century evidence shows a changing religious composition and named mahalles whose Serbian, Albanian and mixed Serb-Albanian labels are kept separate from religion.

The heritage layer is strongest for named urban monuments in Skopje and Prizren and for Alaca Mosque and Harabati Baba Tekke in Tetovo. Several sites survived through rebuilding or adaptive reuse. The register explicitly records lost components such as the original Skopje Bedesten, former components of the Mustafa Paşa complex, the Terzi-Mëhalla cemetery, and ruined Sinani Tekke components.

## Largest evidence gaps

The next acquisition priority is direct extraction of the Ottoman scans and editions: TD 370 and TD 167 pages, the 1832/33 Tetovo and Skopje editions, BOA NFS.d.5624 folios, BOA ML.VRD.TMT.15465, and the year-specific Üsküp/Kalkandelen/Prizren pages of the eight İSAM salnames. The pilot still lacks a defensible village-by-village Ottoman-to-modern crosswalk for the Tetovo rural dossier and for Veşlovik/Yeşlovik, Mavriyani/Mavreva and Pavrova. Historical administrative and cultural-region polygons have not been drawn because the underlying geometry was not extracted.

## Production-readiness

**Conditionally production-ready:** source catalog, resolved city/town gazetteer, heritage records with official/institutional references, and modern point references.

**Research-ready but not production-ready:** Ottoman demographic values pending original-folio verification, Ottoman administrative units pending year-specific geometry extraction, migration totals that cover wider regions, and all historical-map-derived or cultural-region geometries.
