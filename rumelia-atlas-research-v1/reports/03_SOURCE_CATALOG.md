# Rumelia Atlas pilot: Skopje–Tetovo–Prizren source catalog

**Scope.** This catalog covers the corridor linking modern Skopje/Üsküp/Скопје/Shkup, Tetovo/Kalkandelen/Тетово, and Prizren/Призрен, with the wider Ottoman administrative frames that made the corridor legible (Rumeli/Rum-ili, Üsküp/Skopje, Prizren and Kosova vilayets, and the neighbouring Manastır/Selanik systems). It prioritises records that can support historical GIS layers for settlements, households, taxes, waqfs, administrative boundaries, roads, and built heritage. Access was checked **2026-10-01**.

## How to read the tiers

* **Tier A1 — directly verified primary or official evidence.** I opened the scan/catalog/database page and could confirm the title, institutional provenance, and either a usable digital object, a stable record, or a precise archive shelfmark.
* **Tier A2 — directly verified institutional finding aid or official statistical/heritage source.** The source is authoritative and usable, but may require a reading-room request, a data request, or manual export rather than offering an open scan.
* **Tier B — directly verified scholarship with precise primary-source references.** The page or full text was opened and the article/book metadata and source base were checked. Use the scholarship as a guide to the records, not as a substitute for them.
* **Tier C — source discovery lead.** A search result, blocked/dynamic page, or incomplete catalog was located but not sufficiently opened or bibliographically verified. These are acquisition targets, not evidence.

**Reliability is not the same as usability.** A1/A2 official records can be administrative, selective, or politically framed. Ottoman fiscal registers are excellent for taxable households, revenue, land and institutions but do not equal a modern census. Late Ottoman population registers are gender-, religion- and status-specific. Heritage databases document official protection categories and present descriptions, not necessarily the original building chronology.

## Strongest evidence chain for the pilot

1. **Sixteenth-century administrative/fiscal baseline:** the published Ottoman editions of **TD 370 (937/1530), vol. I** for Üsküp, Kalkandelen, Kırçova and adjacent Rum-ili kazas, and **TD 167 (937/1530), vol. II** for Vılçıtrın and Prizren. The latter is the central published tahrir gateway for the Kosovo side of the corridor. [9]
2. **Direct nineteenth-century population records:** **BOA NFS.d.5624**, the 1833 icmal of the 1832 Skopje, Kalkandelen and Kırçova detailed registers, and the North Macedonia State Archives editions of the **1832/33 Tetovo** and **Skopje** population registers. [3] [4] [12]
3. **Household economy and land-use proxy:** **BOA ML.VRD.TMT.15465**, the 1845 Pristina temettuat register, with named household heads, occupations, fields, gardens, shops, inns, mills, income and taxes. [13]
4. **Waqf and urban institutions:** the VGMA Prizren cash-waqf registers cited by Demir, plus the waqf inventories and vakfiye holdings described in the North Macedonia archival guide. [2] [14]
5. **Late Ottoman administrative/toponymic control:** the digitised **Kosova Vilayeti salnames** (1879, 1883, 1885, 1887, 1888, 1893, 1896 and 1900) in İSAM, cross-checked against HathiTrust and the University of Chicago holdings. [5] [6] [7]
6. **Built heritage and modern spatial control:** the Kosovo Cultural Heritage Database records for Prizren’s Mehmed Pasha Mosque and Gazi Mehmed Pasha Hamam, the National Center for Conservation’s central-register mandate in North Macedonia, and official cadastral/geoportal services for modern georeferencing. [23] [25] [26] [27] [31]

## A. Ottoman primary sources, archival guides and source editions

### A1. Ottoman Archives in Kosovo / Kosova Vilayeti — selected documents

* **Author/editor and title:** T.C. Başbakanlık Devlet Arşivleri Genel Müdürlüğü, Osmanlı Arşivi Daire Başkanlığı, **Osmanlı Arşiv Belgelerinde Kosova Vilayeti / Vilajeti i Kosovës në dokumentet arkivore osmane**.
* **Year, publisher, language:** 2007; Ottoman Archive Publications no. 87, İstanbul; Turkish–Albanian bilingual presentation. Project director Yusuf Sarınay; preparation team H. Yıldırım Ağanoğlu, Sebahattin Bayram, Yılmaz Karaca, Ahmet Semih Torun and Mümin Yıldızt­aş. Albanian summaries/translations are credited to Refike Sülçevi of the Kosovo State Archives. [1]
* **Directly verified usable evidence:** The open official PDF reproduces document images and transcriptions. It names the Prizren–Üsküp–Kalkandelen corridor directly: the 1879 Prizren association discussion (printed pp. 38–39) lists the sanjaks of Prizren, Priştine, Üsküp and others; an 1828 military procurement record (printed around pp. 109–111) identifies **BOA C. AS 34340** and purchases from Prizren, Kalkandelen and Üsküp. Other pages reproduce **Y. PRK. MYD, 2/1_3–4** and administrative, political and demographic documents.
* **Use:** A1 primary-document image/transcription collection for late Ottoman administration, political networks, military procurement, migration and place-name crosswalks. It is especially useful for linking Turkish Ottoman forms to Albanian modern names.
* **Caveat:** This is a curated publication, not the complete Kosova Vilayeti fonds. Treat the printed transcription as a finding aid and cite the underlying BOA code when extracting data.
* **URL:** [official PDF](https://www.devletarsivleri.gov.tr/varliklar/dosyalar/eskisiteden/yayinlar/osmanli-arsivi-yayinlar/osmanli_arsiv_belgelerinde_kosova_vilayeti.pdf) [1]
* **Tier:** **A1**.

### A2. Ottoman documents preserved in North Macedonia — archival guide and catalogue

* **Author/editor and title:** T.C. Başbakanlık Devlet Arşivleri Genel Müdürlüğü, Osmanlı Arşivi Daire Başkanlığı, **Makedonya’daki Osmanlı Evrakı**.
* **Year, publisher, language:** 1996; Publication no. 29, Ankara; Turkish, with Macedonian translation credited to Ayten Ardel. Project director İsmet Binark; prepared by Orhan Sakın and Uğurhan Demirbaş. [2]
* **Directly verified usable evidence:** The official PDF describes the holdings of the Skopje State Archives and regional repositories. It reports sixteenth–nineteenth-century local-administration series; **kadı sicilleri** from Debre, Ohrid, Prilep, Kalkandelen and Štip; nineteenth-/early twentieth-century **tapu kütüğü** volumes; municipal and administrative records from **Üsküp and Kalkandelen**; and a special collection of **80 fermans (1469–1916), 153 berats (1702–1911), 28 vakfiyes (1463–1911)** and eight buyruldus. It also records the 1991/1995 microfilm and photocopy transfer made under the Turkish–Macedonian archival cooperation protocol. The guide’s catalogue section gives folder/serial descriptions for municipal, refugee commission, cadastral, tapu and financial records (see especially the holdings discussion around printed pp. 21–22 and the catalogue beginning around p. 74).
* **Use:** Highest-value discovery instrument for the North Macedonian side. Request the **Macedonian State Archives / State Archives of the Republic of North Macedonia** catalogue using the Turkish and Macedonian series names; ask specifically for Kalkandelen/Tetovo, Üsküp/Skopje, municipal, tapu, cadastral and kadı-sicil material.
* **Caveat:** It is a 1996 guide. Shelfmarks, access conditions and the physical location of individual series must be reconfirmed with the present archive.
* **URL:** [official PDF](https://www.devletarsivleri.gov.tr/varliklar/dosyalar/eskisiteden/yayinlar/osmanli-arsivi-yayinlar/makedonyadaki_osmanli_evraki.pdf) [2]
* **Tier:** **A1/A2**.

### A3. North Macedonia State Archives editions: Tetovo/Kalkandelen population registers, 1832/33

* **Author/editor and title:** **Турски документи за историјата на Македонија, пописи од XIX век – Скопски Санџак, Тетовска Каза 1832/33, книга 1, том 1**; Macedonian edition. The archive lists Dr Muzafer Bislimi, Dr Adnan Sherif and M-r Irfete Ibraimi as authors/editors.
* **Year, publisher, language and identifier:** 2019; State Archives of the Republic of North Macedonia; Macedonian edition, **ISBN 978-608-4745-97-6**, 645 pp. A parallel Albanian edition is listed as **Dokumente osmane për historinë e Maqedonisë… kaza Tetovë 1832/1833**, ISBN 978-608-4745-98-3, 625 pp. [4]
* **Directly verified usable evidence:** The official 2021 announcement explains that the edition reproduces the first modern Ottoman population enumeration for the Tetovo kaza’s Muslim population: eight Tetovo neighbourhoods, 66 upper-branch villages, eight villages in the river-gorge branch, 27 lower-branch villages and 14 Dervend villages. The announcement distinguishes these from fifteenth-/sixteenth-century tax surveys and explains the military-versus-tax logic of the nineteenth-century count. [3]
* **Use:** Direct household/person and settlement evidence for Tetovo/Kalkandelen and its rural hinterland; the Albanian and Macedonian editions are valuable for name, locality and terminology cross-checking. Photograph or transcribe the original Ottoman pages where possible rather than relying only on editorial transliteration.
* **Caveat:** The announcement does not expose the original archive shelfmark or a digital scan. Treat the printed edition as an official documentary edition and request the underlying register and editorial protocol from the State Archives.
* **URLs:** [archive publication catalogue](https://arhiv.mk/publikuvanje-2/) [4]; [official announcement](https://arhiv.mk/2021/02/02/%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%B6%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%85%D0%B8%D0%B2-%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%98%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B8-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE-%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5/) [3]
* **Tier:** **A1/A2**.

### A4. North Macedonia State Archives edition: Skopje kaza, 1832/33

* **Author/editor and title:** **Османлиски документи за историјата на Македонија – Скопски Санџак, Каза – Скопје 1832/1833, том 1, дел 1**; Adnan Sherif, Muzafer Bislimi and Irfete Ibraimi.
* **Year, publisher, language and identifier:** 2020; State Archives of the Republic of North Macedonia; Macedonian edition, **ISBN 978-608-4900-05-4**, 639 pp. The catalogue also lists an Albanian edition, **Dokumente Osmane… Sanxaku i Shkupit, Kaza Shkup, 1832/1833**, ISBN **978-608-4900-06-1**, 604 pp. [4]
* **Use:** Official edition for the Skopje kaza; pair it with BOA NFS.d.5624 (A8 below) and the 1833 demographic study (B2) to audit transcription, settlement names, religious categories and household counts.
* **Caveat:** The public catalogue gives publication metadata but not the original Ottoman shelfmark. Obtain the scan, editorial introduction and index from the State Archives before treating the edition as a machine-readable dataset.
* **URL:** [State Archives publication catalogue](https://arhiv.mk/publikuvanje-2/) [4]
* **Tier:** **A1/A2**.

### A5. Digitised **Kosova Vilayeti Salnamesi** — İSAM record D02460

* **Title/publisher/language:** **Kosova Vilayeti Salnâmesi**, printed by the Kosova Vilâyet press; Ottoman Turkish. The page is an İSAM digital-series record, identifier **D02460**.
* **Directly verified volumes:** İSAM exposes eight scan links: **1296/1879 (1st issue), 1300/1883 (2nd), 1302/1885 (3rd), 1304/1887 (4th), 1305/1888 (5th), 1311/1893 (6th), 1314/1896 (7th), 1318/1900 (8th)**. The page gives the stable PDF codes and file sizes, e.g. `D02460129600000001`, `D02460130000000002`, `D02460131400000007` and `D02460131800000008`. [5]
* **Use:** High-value annual administrative source for vilayet/sanjak/kaza boundaries, officials, institutions, roads, population tables, education, public works and place names. For the pilot, extract the Üsküp, Kalkandelen and Prizren entries from at least 1879/1883/1893/1896/1900 and build a controlled Ottoman-to-modern toponym table.
* **Caveat:** Salnames are official yearbooks. Their population tables reflect the state’s categories and administrative purpose; they are not interchangeable with the 1832/33 nüfus registers or a modern census.
* **URL:** [İSAM series and PDF links](https://isamveri.org/salname/sayilar.php?sidno=d02460) [5]
* **Tier:** **A1**.

### A6. HathiTrust catalog and full-view scans of the Kosova salname

* **Catalog record:** **Salname-yi Vilâyet-i Kosova / سالنامئى ولايت قوصوه**; Ottoman Turkish; Kosova [Serbia], Kosova vilâyet matbaası; HathiTrust record **003515309**, OCLC **427540658**.

### A8. Institute for Mediterranean Studies — Balkan provincial salname collection

* **Institution/title:** Institute for Mediterranean Studies (IMS), Foundation for Research and Technology–Hellas, **Salname (almanacs) of the Balkan and island provinces of the Ottoman Empire**; Ottoman Turkish, digitised collection.
* **Directly verified holdings:** The IMS page states that its library acquired 87 rare second-half-nineteenth-century volumes, including **Selanik (19 vols., H. 1287–1325 / 1870–1907), Yanya (8 vols., H. 1288–1319 / 1871–1901) and Manastır (9 vols., H. 1292–1314 / 1875–1896)**. The material is available for study in the IMS library. [8]
* **Use:** Comparative provincial-yearbook control for the neighbouring Manastır/Selanik systems and for checking routes, officials, institutions and administrative terminology against Kosova salnames.
* **Caveat:** The verified collection page does not list Kosova volumes; use İSAM/HathiTrust/UChicago for Kosova itself.
* **URL:** [IMS collection page](https://ims.forth.gr/en/page/view?id=47) [8]
* **Tier:** **A2**.

### A9. BOA NFS.d.5624 — 1833 Skopje population register

* **Record:** **NFS.d.5624**, an icmal of detailed population registers for **Üsküp, Kalkandelen and Kırçova kazas**, headed in Ottoman Turkish as a register of Muslim subjects, reaya, Jews, Kıptîs and foreigners in those kazas.
* **Direct verification route:** The exact shelfmark, title and scope are reproduced from the register by Hatice Akın Zorba in a peer-reviewed article. The register summarises the 1832 detailed registers and covers the Skopje centre and villages, religious categories and cizye classes. [12]
* **Use:** The best directly identified Ottoman archival record for corridor-wide nineteenth-century demographic structure. It can be used to connect Skopje to Tetovo/Kalkandelen in one administrative document and to test the State Archives editions.
* **Caveat:** The scan was not found open on the BOA website during this pass. Request a BOA digital copy or reading-room consultation by the exact code **NFS.d.5624**; preserve folio/page references in the pilot database.
* **Primary-language form:** Ottoman Turkish; scholarly analysis in Turkish and English abstract.
* **Tier:** **A2 primary record, accessed through Tier B scholarship**.

### A10. BOA ML.VRD.TMT.15465 — 1845 Pristina temettuat register

* **Record:** **ML.VRD.TMT.15465**, Maliye Nezareti Varidat Kalemi Temettuat Defterleri; title identifies the 1845 register of Muslim, non-Muslim and Kıptî households in the Pristina town in the Üsküp eyalet.
* **Direct verification route:** Öztürk and Karaçam Atam report that the register is 18 × 50 cm, 260 pages, with usable entries on printed pp. **4–246**; pages 1–3, 177–181 and 247–260 are blank or without registered information. It records household-head names, occupations, land, gardens, shops, inns, mills, income and taxes. [13]
* **Use:** A rare bridge between demographic and economic GIS: household points or neighbourhood aggregates, occupations, land-use proxies and fiscal inequality. It is especially useful for comparing the Pristina market sphere with Skopje/Tetovo.
* **Caveat:** Temettuat is a tax/wealth enumeration and town-centred. Do not treat it as a total population census or infer household size without an explicit model.
* **Tier:** **A2 primary record, accessed through Tier B scholarship**.

### A11. Published tahrir/muhasebe editions: TD 370 and TD 167

* **TD 370 (937/1530), vol. I:** **370 Numaralı Muhâsebe-i Vilâyet-i Rûm-ili Defteri (937/1530), I: Paşa (Sofya) ve Vize Livâları ile Sağkol Kazâları**; T.C. Başbakanlık Devlet Arşivleri Genel Müdürlüğü, Ankara, **2001**. The published contents explicitly include **Üsküb, Kalkan-delen, Kırçova, Manastır, Pirlepe and Köprülü**, along with other Rum-ili kazas. [9]
* **TD 167 (937/1530), vol. II:** **167 Numaralı Muhâsebe-i Vilâyet-i Rûm-ili Defteri (937/1530) II: Vılçıtrın, Prizrin, Alaca-hisâr ve Hersek Livâları**; T.C. Başbakanlık Devlet Arşivleri Genel Müdürlüğü, Ankara, **2004**. This is the key published primary edition for the Prizren side. [9]
* **Language and access:** Ottoman Turkish source text with modern Turkish editorial apparatus/index; the UConn bibliography is an English-language finding aid, and the Turkish State Archives maintains the official edition PDFs. The official TD 370 PDF URL was opened, but the web extractor returned the binary PDF stream rather than readable metadata; bibliographic details are therefore cross-checked to the UConn bibliography and should be checked against the title page of the download before final citation.
* **Use:** Build the sixteenth-century place-name, village, household, timar/revenue and institution layers from the edition indexes and facsimile pages. Use TD 370 I for Skopje/Tetovo/Kırçova and TD 167 II for Prizren/Vılçıtrın, preserving defter and printed-page references.
* **URLs:** [UConn published-tahrir bibliography](https://ottoman.uconn.edu/bibliography/published-tahrirs/) [9]; [official TD 370 PDF](https://www.devletarsivleri.gov.tr/varliklar/dosyalar/eskisiteden/yayinlar/osmanli-arsivi-yayinlar/370_nolu_muhasebei_rum_ili_defteri.pdf)
* **Tier:** **A1/A2**.

## B. Directly verified academic books, articles and dissertations

### B1. Tatjana Katıć, “The Sancak of Prizren in the 15th and 16th century”

* **Citation:** Tatjana Katıć, “The Sancak of Prizren in the 15th and 16th century,” *OTAM Ankara Üniversitesi Osmanlı Tarihi Araştırma ve Uygulama Merkezi Dergisi* 33 (2013): **113–138**, DOI **10.1501/OTAM_0000000617**. Language: English title/abstract with Turkish journal metadata. [10]
* **Directly verified source base:** BOA Maliyeden Müdevver Defterleri **12, 34, 656, 7534**; BOA Tapu Tahrir Defterleri **5m, 92, 368, 495, 499**; Istanbul Atatürk Library Muallim Cevdet Evrakı **36-03**; Tapu ve Kadastro Kuyûd-ı Kadîme Arşivi Ankara **TK 55**; and the 2004 TD 167 vol. II edition. [10]
* **Use:** The best verified scholarly roadmap for sixteenth-century Prizren administrative geography, settlement, migration, religious/demographic change and economy. Its archival list is immediately actionable for a BOA/TKKA request.
* **URL:** [DergiPark full metadata and PDF](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/otam/article/132237) [10]
* **Tier:** **B (high; precise archival references)**.

### B2. Hatice Akın Zorba, “1833 Tarihli Nüfus Defterine Göre Üsküp Kazası’nın Demografik Yapısı”

* **Citation:** Hatice Akın Zorba, “1833 Tarihli Nüfus Defterine Göre Üsküp Kazası’nın Demografik Yapısı / The Demographic Structure of Skopje from the 1833 Register,” *Mediterranean Journal of Humanities* VIII/1 (2018): **23–40**, DOI **10.13114/MJH.2018.380**. Turkish with English title/abstract. [12]
* **Directly verified content:** The article identifies BOA **NFS.d.5624**, reproduces its Ottoman title, explains that it is an icmal of the detailed 1832 registers for Üsküp, Kalkandelen and Kırçova, and analyses Skopje’s 47 neighbourhoods, villages, Muslim/non-Muslim/Jewish/Kıptî categories and cizye classes. The article is the most useful open scholarly bridge to the unscanned register.
* **Use:** Use its tables and page-level citations as a checksum, then re-read the register. It is particularly useful for defining what the register does and does not count.
* **URL:** [DergiPark PDF](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3679047) [12]
* **Tier:** **B (high; exact shelfmark and method)**.

### B3. Gülin Öztürk and Şenay Karaçam Atam, “19. yüzyılın ortalarında Priştine”

* **Citation:** Gülin Öztürk and Şenay Karaçam Atam, **“Temettuat Defterlerine Göre 19. Yüzyılın Ortalarında Priştine / Pristina in the Middle of the 19th Century in the Record Books of Economy,”** *TÜBAR* XXX (2011 Güz), printed article begins at **p. 284**; Turkish with English abstract. [13]
* **Directly verified content:** The opened PDF identifies BOA **ML.VRD.TMT.15465**, printed pp. 4–246 of the register, and explains the household, occupation, property, agricultural and tax fields. It also cites BOA HAT 339/19419 for Pristina’s administrative attachment to Üsküp and several HAT/C DH references for the Yaşar Paşa/Abdurrahman Paşa administration.
* **Use:** A model for extracting temettuat variables and for separating town households from the wider kaza. Do not copy the article’s historical narrative without checking its cited archival documents.
* **URL:** [stable PDF](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/157094) [13]
* **Tier:** **B**.

### B4. Ahmet Demir, “Place of Cash Waqfs in the Balkan Heritage of the Ottoman State: Prizren Example”

### B5. Eyüp Kul and Onur Pehlivan, “Tahrir Defterlerine Göre 15. ve 16. Yüzyıllarda Üsküp…”

* **Citation:** Eyüp Kul and Onur Pehlivan, **“Tahrir Defterlerine Göre 15. ve 16. Yüzyıllarda Üsküp’ün Mahalle Yapısı ve Mekânsal Gelişimi,”** *Kent Akademisi* 19/5 (2026): **1–24**, DOI **10.35674/kent.1922461**. Turkish. [15]
* **Directly verified source base and method:** the study uses tahrir records dated **1455, 1468, 1529, 1544 and 1569**, and retroactively compares neighbourhood names with the 1890 Kosova Vilayeti salname city plan. It explicitly warns that missing tahrir data, loss of Skopje şer‘iyye sicilleri, the 1689 Austrian burning and migration prevent exact placement of many neighbourhoods.
* **Use:** Useful for a first historical-neighbourhood layer and for an explicit methodological warning against over-precise geolocation. Cross-check its tabulations against the published defters and the salname scans.
* **URL:** [DergiPark full metadata/PDF](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/kent/article/1922461) [15]
* **Tier:** **B**.

### B6. Mehmet İnbaşı, “The Kaza of Kalkandelen (Tetovo) (1455–1569)”

* **Citation:** Mehmet İnbaşı, **“The Kaza of Kalkandelen (Tetovo) (1455–1569),”** *Turkish Review of Balkan Studies* 6 (İSİS, İstanbul, 2001): **17–36**. English title/abstract and figures; the verified landing page is an Academia repository copy.
* **Directly verified content:** The opened record describes Kalkandelen’s changing status from vilayet/nahiye to kaza, population change between 1455 and 1569, Muslim/non-Muslim settlement, occupations, trade, taxation, timars and vakıfs. The repository page identifies a 22-page PDF and displays tables/figures.
* **Use:** The most direct corridor-specific academic lead for Tetovo before the nineteenth-century registers. Use it to identify the tahrirs and village/mahalle names, then obtain the published defter or BOA images.
* **Caveat:** Academia is a hosting platform rather than the publisher; verify the journal’s original scan and page numbering before publication.
* **URL:** [repository copy](https://www.academia.edu/32620749/Mehmet_%C4%B0nba%C5%9F%C4%B1_THE_KAZA_OF_KALKANDELEN_Tetovo_1455_1569_Turkish_Review_of_Balkan_Studies_ISIS_Istanbul_2001_6_pp_17_36)
* **Tier:** **B (metadata/full-text landing page verified; publisher copy to confirm)**.

### B7. Machiel Kiel, “Prizren” in TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi

## Language and name-control strategy

Search and record all names in parallel. At minimum, use:

* **Ottoman Turkish/Turkish:** Üsküp/Üsküb, Kalkandelen, Prizren/Prizrin, Priştine, Kosova Vilayeti, Rum-ili/Rumeli, kaza, sancak/liva, tahrir, nüfus, temettuat, salname, vakıf/vakfiye, kadı sicili, tapu.
* **Macedonian:** Скопје, Тетово, Призрен, османлиски документи, пописи, Скопски санџак, Тетовска каза, Државен архив.
* **Albanian:** Shkup, Tetovë, Prizren, Prishtinë, sanxhaku i Shkupit, kaza e Tetovës, regjistrime, dokumente osmane, trashëgimia kulturore.
* **Serbian:** Skoplje/Скопље, Tetovo/Тетово, Prizren/Призрен, Priština/Приштина, Kosovska vilajet, turski defteri, katastar.
* **German/French:** Üsküb/Skopje, Kalkandelen, Prizren, Kosovo-Vilajet, osmanische Kataster/Steuerregister/Volkszählungen, Macédoine turque, Kosovo ottoman, cartes ethnographiques/commerciales.

Normalize every place with: original form; transliteration; modern official form; historical administrative unit; latitude/longitude source; confidence; and the exact page/folio or map sheet. Never collapse **Kalkandelen/Tetovo**, **Üsküp/Skopje**, **Prizren/Prizrin** or **Priştine/Prishtinë** into one name without retaining the original form.

## Recommended acquisition and extraction order

1. Download all eight **D02460** Kosova salname scans and create a page-level OCR/transcription log, retaining Ottoman pagination and the İSAM PDF code.
2. Obtain TD 370 I and TD 167 II; extract only corridor-relevant entries first, with defter page and index references.
3. Request **BOA NFS.d.5624** and the underlying State Archives 1832/33 Tetovo and Skopje registers; compare the official Macedonian/Albanian editions against images.
4. Request **BOA ML.VRD.TMT.15465** for Pristina and use the 1845 article’s pp. 4–246 guide to sample household and property fields.
5. Request Katıć’s cited BOA/TKKA series and Demir’s VGMA defters by exact code/page/sıra; prioritise waqf founders, shops, mills, roads, waterworks and neighbourhoods.
6. Ask the North Macedonia State Archives and NI-RIPCM for object-level records for Skopje/Tetovo Ottoman monuments; use the Kosovo database’s unique IDs as the model for a unified heritage schema.
7. Build modern control layers from ASKData, North Macedonia SSO, KCA Geoportal and the LOC map collections, clearly flagging post-Ottoman territorial changes.
8. Only then add discovery leads (David Rumsey, OldMapsOnline, municipal catalogues, German/French works) after item-level verification.

## Bottom line

The corridor can be supported by an unusually coherent source stack: **published sixteenth-century tahrirs (TD 370/167), an explicitly identified 1833 BOA nüfus register (NFS.d.5624), official 1832/33 Macedonian archival editions for Skopje and Tetovo, a precisely cited 1845 Pristina temettuat register, digitised Kosova salnames, VGMA waqf references, official monument records, and modern statistical/cadastral/map controls**. The principal unresolved acquisition gaps are open access to the BOA NFS and ML.VRD originals, object-level North Macedonia heritage records, historical cadastral sheets, and the unverified municipal/German/French discovery leads. No Wikipedia or blog page was used as evidence.
