# Known limitations

1. The pilot is not a complete inventory of all villages, quarters or monuments. It is a conservative first release.
2. Ottoman fiscal, nüfus, temettuat and salname records measure different objects and must not be merged as if they were one census.
3. Several demographic rows are reported through scholarship and require original-folio checks before public display as exact counts.
4. Current coordinates are place or building reference points, not surveyed historical boundaries.
5. No ethnicity is inferred from religion, names, a mosque, language, or Ottoman legal category.
6. Historical names with uncertain dates remain undated.
7. Migration totals often cover Kosovo, Macedonia, Vardar Banovina, the southern regions or Yugoslavia rather than the three pilot towns.
8. Historical maps have varying and often unknown reuse rights.
9. Cultural-region and migration geometries are claim-only/null geometry until source maps or archival route evidence are digitized.
10. Kosovo and North Macedonia labels follow current reference usage while preserving source wording; the package does not make a territorial or political claim.
